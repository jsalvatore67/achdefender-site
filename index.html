How it works

A checkpoint between your clients and the ACH network.

1

Payroll is queued

Your payroll platform prepares the debit as usual. Nothing about your workflow changes.

2

ACH Defender checks it

The client's balance is verified in real time and the run is compared against their normal behavior.

3

Clear, or flag and alert

Healthy debits go through. Risky ones are flagged and your team is alerted before the ACH is initiated.
